HRCA careers page

Our site search and internal search data kept surfacing the same unmet need: people wanted a careers page that did not exist, so all we could offer was a link out to a third-party job board. I saw the opportunity, made the case for building our own, and then shaped the content strategy, wrote the copy, and designed and built the page myself. It gives HRCA a careers destination it owns and keeps job seekers on the site instead of sending them elsewhere.
